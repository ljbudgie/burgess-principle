# Case study: TV Licensing / BBC

**Sector:** Public broadcaster / enforcement  
**Finding:** Automated letters at the outset; the public register currently records AMBIGUOUS  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Period:** 2025

## What happened

Automated enforcement letters were sent because no television licence was recorded for an address. The letters used a threatening standard form. There was no evidence at the outset that a named person had checked whether a licence was actually required.

## The test

The question asked was whether a named human with authority had reviewed the specific facts of the address before the letters were sent.

## Outcome

The letters ceased. The address was recorded as not requiring a licence for a stated period. The public institutional register currently records TV Licensing / BBC as AMBIGUOUS, because human involvement was not fully evidenced even though the enforcement stopped.
