## Governing question

> "Was a named human being's mind applied to the specific facts of a specific person's case before institutional power was exercised?"

- **SOVEREIGN** — the condition is met
- **NULL** — the starting point: the condition is not yet met

The registered standard is binary. AMBIGUOUS is a correspondence working note used when a reply has not yet answered the question. It is not a certification determination under the regulations.

Certification is for organisations that can evidence the SOVEREIGN condition in respect of the service class for which certification is held. The ledger records certified adoption; it is not a substitute for actually doing the review.

Certification is granted based on evidence, not payment. The Burgess Principle Ltd may charge the registered fees for assessment and renewal, but payment does not confer certification and cannot convert a NULL determination into a SOVEREIGN determination.

If a decision has already affected you, **you do not need certification**.

## Registered tiers

Authorised Users are allocated to a tier on the basis of organisational type and annual revenue, as set out in section 7 of the registered regulations. Fees are from section 13.1. All fees are stated exclusive of VAT (section 13.2).

| Tier | For | Application fee | Annual reassessment fee | Apply |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1 — Global Technology** | Organisations with annual global revenue exceeding £1 billion, including major technology, financial services and multinational organisations | £5,000 – £50,000 | £5,000 – £25,000 | [Apply](https://certify.theburgessprinciple.com/apply/?tier=tier1) |
| **Tier 2 — Corporate** | Organisations with annual revenue between £1 million and £1 billion, including corporate entities, professional services firms and commercial operators | £500 – £3,000 | £500 – £2,000 | [Apply](https://certify.theburgessprinciple.com/apply/?tier=tier2) |
| **Tier 3 — Public Sector** | UK public bodies, including local authorities, NHS trusts, government departments, regulators and other public sector organisations | Nil | Nil | [Apply](https://certify.theburgessprinciple.com/apply/?tier=tier3) |
| **Tier 4 — Litigation Support** | Organisations or individuals seeking to use the framework in legal proceedings, regulatory challenges or dispute resolution | By negotiation | By negotiation | [Apply](https://certify.theburgessprinciple.com/apply/?tier=tier4) |

Tier 3 certification is provided at nil cost as a matter of public accountability policy. The official source is section 13.1 of the registered regulations, which sets both the application fee and the annual reassessment fee at Nil. There is no payment link for Tier 3.

The exact fee within a registered range is confirmed in writing before assessment begins. Payment is never taken from the apply form.

### Tier 1 — Global Technology

- Full systems audit of automated decision-making infrastructure across the relevant service classes
- Evidence of human review checkpoints and a sampling method for high-volume decisions
- Annual reassessment by a senior Qualified Assessor
- Quarterly returns required

### Tier 2 — Corporate

- Document review and process audit of decision-making operations in the certified service class
- Evidence of individual human review and named reviewer designation
- Annual reassessment
- Quarterly returns required

### Tier 3 — Public Sector

- Document review and process audit of public-facing automated decision processes in the certified service class
- Same evidence standard as every other registered tier
- Annual reassessment, at nil cost
- Quarterly returns required where decision volume warrants

### Tier 4 — Litigation Support

- Targeted assessment of the specific decision or process under challenge
- A documented SOVEREIGN or NULL determination, with reasoning and evidence, in a form suitable for correspondence, regulatory submissions or legal proceedings
- Fees and scope agreed in writing before assessment begins

## Neutral oversight

The Burgess Principle Ltd is the registered proprietor of the Mark and administers certification separately from ordinary MIT reuse. Assessments are carried out by Qualified Assessors appointed by the Proprietor, under section 11 of the registered regulations.

To preserve impartiality:

- certification is granted, renewed, refused, suspended or withdrawn against the published evidence standard, not the applicant's commercial value;
- assessment records should identify the scope reviewed, the evidence relied on, the named accountable person, and any NULL determinations;
- any material commercial, personal or partnership interest between the company and the applicant should be recorded before the certification decision;
- where that interest could reasonably affect perceived neutrality, the Proprietor's policy is that an assessment should be carried out by a Qualified Assessor who is independent of that interest, or referred to an external verifier, before the Mark is granted or renewed.

Paid support may help an organisation prepare, but it must not be treated as independent certification of the same evidence unless the conflict is recorded and mitigated.
