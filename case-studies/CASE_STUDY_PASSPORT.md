# Case study: HM Passport Office

**Sector:** Government / identity documents  
**Finding:** NULL at issuance  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Period:** 2026

## What happened

A passport was issued through a centralised application and biometric-processing pipeline. The instrument did not show that a named reviewer had considered the specific application before issue.

## The test

Can a core identity document be issued without any named human being visible on the decision?

## Outcome

A subject-access request, an automated-decision challenge, and an Equality Act challenge were sent after issue. The public register records HMPO as NULL.
