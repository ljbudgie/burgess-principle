# Named-mind index

The institutional register scores a body. The binary test scores a person.

A unit signature, a "we", or a role without a name is not a mind on the file.
A named officer who puts one sentence on the file is an exhibit. Keep the
sentence. Do not restate the week.

This file is not a private case dump and not a replacement for
[`INSTITUTIONAL_REGISTER.md`](./INSTITUTIONAL_REGISTER.md). It is the missing
unit: the officer.

Standing language for the letter itself: [`REGISTER.md`](./REGISTER.md).

## Four patterns that recur

These are reusable. The names below are the specimens, not the only instances.

### 1. Dual role

The same named officer authorises the enforcement spend and decides the
welfare question on the accounts in that spend.

Specimen: Wendy Tarelli, Revenues and Benefits Manager, Darlington Borough
Council. FOI DBC-4186-26 (11 September 2026) names her as the authoriser of
the £990 HMCTS line on 28 May 2026, "Application fees for Council Tax
Liability Orders." The same officer is the decision-maker on Council Tax
Support and section 13A on the live account file.

Why it matters: that is an arrangement, not a policy preference. The NAO
Code language below is the test for the appointed auditor.

### 2. Subsection swap

The officer agrees the Act gives a power, then quotes a different subsection
to stop using it.

Specimen: Baldev Singh, Revenues and Benefits Team Leader. Emails of 10 and
11 September 2026. He accepted section 13A as a statutory power to reduce the
amount payable, quoted section 13A(2) (the duty to make a reduction scheme),
and wrote: "The Section 13a Discretionary Relief will not be dealt with, until
the Council Tax Support review has been completed." Individual relief is
section 13A(1)(c). He also accepted both emails as the Council's written
response to the appeal.

Why it matters: a policy precondition the Act does not contain, in the
officer's own name, on the section 16 clock.

### 3. Minimum term treated as an ending

A date that ends a commitment period is written as if it ended the contract.

Specimen: Matthew Brown, Customer and Legal Manager, The Bannatyne Group.
DAR2380553, 11 September 2026. Membership started 9 January 2025. Minimum
term ended 9 January 2026 and then rolled. Written cancellation 3 September
2026. He sets termination at 1 November 2026 and admits there is no historic
audit of a specific Terms and Conditions version against an acceptance
timestamp. Last visit on the extract: 7 December 2025.

Why it matters: January is not a discharge. The missing timestamp is the
exhibit on the terms themselves.

### 4. Role without a name

A decision is admitted. The officer who approved it is given only as a rank.
The letter is signed by a unit.

Specimen: Hampshire and Isle of Wight Constabulary, FOI HC/01/FOI/26/019981/V,
11 September 2026. Inclusion Matters linked to pay progression. Agreed by the
People Board. Approved by the Deputy Chief Constable. No Equality Impact
Assessment. Signed "Public Access Joint Information Management Unit."

Why it matters: a pay-progression link without a named approver and without
an EIA is AMBIGUOUS until the name exists. Internal review is the next act,
not a second essay.

## Code language worth keeping

Suzy Smith, Senior Audit Manager, Local Audit Code and Guidance, National
Audit Office. Letter 11 September 2026 on Darlington liability-order lists.

She will not re-audit a council and she does not hold a council file. She did
give the appointed-auditor test in her own words:

auditors may examine the arrangements by which policy decisions are reached
and consider the effects of the implementation of policy.

A significant weakness, in that letter, includes exposure to financial loss or
risk, impact on service or reputation, and arrangements that could reasonably
be expected to lead to unlawful action. Operational work stays with the
appointed auditor.

Map pattern 1 onto that sentence. Do not ask the NAO to keep a Darlington
file a second time.
