# Case study: Automated affordability review at a digital lender

**Sector:** Financial services / lending  
**Finding:** NULL, then SOVEREIGN after escalation  
**Status:** Worked example  
**Period:** 2026

This is a worked example of the test in automated underwriting. It does not publish applicant details, amounts, or contact addresses.

## What happened

A UK FCA-regulated digital lender used automated underwriting to assess affordability. After an offer was issued, the applicant asked whether a named person had reviewed their individual circumstances, including variable self-employed income and a recent change in household costs, or whether the decision was the output of the algorithm alone.

The first reply described the automated system and pointed the applicant to a generic review channel.

## The test

CONC expects individual judgment about affordability. The binary test asks whether that judgment was actually applied to this applicant before the offer was made.

## Outcome

After escalation, a named review was recorded and the process was redesigned. The worked example shows the move from NULL to SOVEREIGN once a real person looks at the specific facts.
