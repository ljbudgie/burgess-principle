# Case study: Equifax

**Sector:** Credit reference  
**Finding:** NULL  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Period:** 2026

## What happened

Two County Court Judgments were recorded on a credit file. When the entries were challenged, and the company was asked who had reviewed them before registration, the matter was closed through an automated no-reply message that directed the person to web and phone channels, despite a written request for email-only communication.

## The test

Did anyone with authority review the specific entries, and the restriction request, before they were recorded or before the file was closed?

## Outcome

The automated closure did not name a reviewer and did not engage with the substance of the challenge. The public register records Equifax as NULL.
