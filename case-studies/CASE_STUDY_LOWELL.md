# Case study: Lowell Financial Ltd

**Sector:** Purchased consumer debt  
**Finding:** NULL  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Period:** 2026

## What happened

A purchased energy debt was pursued through a standard recovery pipeline. The challenge asked whether a named human with authority had reviewed the specific account before recovery action continued.

## The test

Was a named person applying their mind to this account, or was the file being processed as one of a batch?

## Outcome

The complaint was not upheld. Recovery nevertheless ceased and the account was closed in April 2026. A purchased-debt workflow may be abandoned once a structured challenge forces the record beyond automation, even without a concession on the binary test.
