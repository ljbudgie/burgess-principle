# Case study: Bannatyne Group Limited

**Sector:** Leisure / consumer contract  
**Finding:** NULL on the contract documents  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Period:** September 2026

## What happened

A written membership cancellation was acknowledged. The same day, a sales reply relied on a contractual obligation and pointed to the live website terms, rather than producing the agreement and terms that were current when the membership was formed. A senior officer later said that one person would be appointed to deal with the file, asked that nobody else at the company be contacted, and did not name that person or attach the original agreement.

## The test

Had a named human with authority reviewed the specific membership documents, or was collection being asserted from a current web page?

## Outcome

The original agreement was not produced. A named person on a channel is not the same as a named person on the documents. The finding recorded here is NULL on collection from a current URL, and unresolved on the promised single-officer channel until a name and a review of the file exist.

This is a correspondence specimen. It is not a court finding.
