# Standing register

The Burgess test is one question. The standing register is how the letter
stays short enough to send, and still counts as an exhibit.

A register is a variety of language that belongs to a situation, not to a
person (Halliday: field, tenor, mode). Lock the situation. The wording
can then stay still.

The institutional register scores a body. The named-mind index keeps the
officer who signed. Use both. Do not merge them.

See [NAMED_MIND.md](./NAMED_MIND.md) for dual role, subsection swap,
minimum term, and role-without-name — the four patterns from the live files.

## The three knobs

| Knob | Setting | If it moves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Field** | This file. This ask. | The letter becomes the week. |
| **Tenor** | I, to a named officer. | *We* returns. Nobody owns the sentence. |
| **Mode** | Written email that *is* the act. | The letter becomes a prelude to a call. |

## The three lines

Paste at the end of the letter:

> I will treat a reply as a response only if it names the person who read these facts and whether they had authority to decide.
> An auto-reply or a "we" holding note is NULL. The clock on this request is [date].
> I will not restate this email. If I escalate, this email is the exhibit.

On first contact with an institution, add one line above them:

> Email only is a reasonable adjustment under the Equality Act 2010.

Do not add a diagnosis. If a named officer asks why the channel is
required, one sentence is enough: you are disabled for the purposes of
the Act, and phone or portal is the disadvantage.

Close, when you want the method visible:

> I reviewed this. I decided this. I am accountable for these sentences.

## How to fill [date]

Borrow the sector clock. Do not invent a shorter one.

| Kind of request | Conservative clock |
| --- | --- |
| Subject access request | 1 calendar month from receipt |
| FOI / EIR | 20 working days |
| Energy / FOS-style complaint | 8 weeks from the first written complaint |
| Named-human acknowledgement only | 10 working days |
| Minister, MP, press | Request 10 working days. Not a statutory duty. |

Write the end date in the letter once. NULL days count inside the clock.
An auto-reply is not a response. A "we have received" note is not a
response. The original email remains the exhibit.

## What this is not

It is not a private SLA that binds every recipient. It is the situation
you will keep, so that a NULL reply cannot pull a restatement out of you.

It is not legal advice. It helps you prepare for meaningful human review.
