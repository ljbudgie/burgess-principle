# Start Here — Path 1: You received a decision or a letter

> “Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific facts of this specific person's case?”

- **SOVEREIGN** — a named human personally reviewed the specific facts before the decision.
- **NULL** — no individual human review took place.
- **AMBIGUOUS** — the reply does not clearly confirm individual review of the specific facts.

**NULL or AMBIGUOUS is a documented starting point for escalation, not a final verdict.**

## Ask in writing

Start with one email. For SOVEREIGN, ask for all five evidence elements:

1. The human reviewer's name.
2. Their role.
3. The specific facts they reviewed about your case.
4. Confirmation that review happened before the decision.
5. Confirmation they had authority to change the outcome.

A general statement about “human oversight” does not supply these details.

## Copy this letter

Replace the bracketed details. Send it to the organisation using the email
address on its letter or official website.

> Subject: Request for individual human review — [reference]
>
> Dear [organisation or team],
>
> I am writing about [decision or letter] dated [date], reference [reference].
>
> Please confirm whether a human member of your team was able to personally
> review the specific facts of my case before this decision was made.
>
> Please provide:
> 1. The reviewer's name.
> 2. Their role.
> 3. The specific facts about my case that they reviewed.
> 4. When the review happened, and confirmation it preceded the decision.
> 5. Confirmation they had authority to change the outcome.
>
> If no individual human review took place, please say so clearly.
> If it did not, or you cannot confirm it, please arrange individual review
> by a named human with authority to change the outcome.
>
> Please reply by email.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> [your name]

Keep your sent email, proof of sending, and every reply privately.
Do not post your case details publicly.

**You can leave this repository now.** Once you have copied the letter,
Path 1 does not need this repository, Python, Iris, certification, or Git.
