# Iris limits and does not do

Iris is an open-source interface to the Burgess Principle. The UK certification mark UK00004343685 protects the name and badge. It does not give Iris legal force against a bailiff, debt collector, or council. It does not prove that any letter Iris drafts is reliable.

The filed regulations require evidence of named human review before certification is granted. Payment does not convert a NULL finding into SOVEREIGN. Iris itself is not a named human reviewer.

A dated email remains the exhibit. The mark is a separate commercial instrument for organisations that choose to adopt the standard.
