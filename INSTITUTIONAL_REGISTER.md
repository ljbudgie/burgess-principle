# Institutional Register

**Updated:** 21 November 2026 (Europe/London)  
**UK Certification Mark:** UK00004343685

This is the public, organisation-level record of institutions assessed against the Burgess Test:

*Was a named human being's mind applied to the specific facts of a specific person's case before institutional power was exercised?*

The authoritative machine-readable file is [`institutional_register.csv`](./institutional_register.csv). This page is a readable copy of that file. It names organisations only. It does not name individuals, and it does not include email addresses, telephone numbers, amounts, or case or reference numbers.

Person-level findings are kept privately. See [`NAMED_MIND.md`](./NAMED_MIND.md).

The scoring method is defined in [`INSTITUTION_AUDIT_TAXONOMY.md`](./INSTITUTION_AUDIT_TAXONOMY.md). The public register publishes the overall finding and score, not the per-dimension breakdown.

## How to read a finding

- **SOVEREIGN** — a named human with authority reviewed the specific facts before the decision was made.
- **NULL** — that review is not evidenced.
- **AMBIGUOUS** — human involvement is claimed but not evidenced for the specific case.
- **PENDING** — assessment is not complete.
- **REMOVED** — a previous certification or listing has been removed.
- Completeness notes such as **NULL (partial)** record that the assessment is incomplete, not a different test.

A finding is a record of whether named human review was evidenced in the correspondence logged. It is not a finding of wrongdoing.
