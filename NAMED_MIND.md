# Named-mind index

The named-mind register, which records named individuals and person-level findings, is kept privately. It is not published in this repository, on the public site, or in the public CSV files.

The public record is organisation-level only:

- [`institutional_register.csv`](./institutional_register.csv) — organisations, sectors, findings and scores
- [`live_findings_ledger.csv`](./live_findings_ledger.csv) — chronological organisation-level events (year and month only)
- [`INSTITUTIONAL_REGISTER.md`](./INSTITUTIONAL_REGISTER.md) and [`LIVE_AUDIT_LOG.md`](./LIVE_AUDIT_LOG.md) — the same record in readable form

The binary test still asks whether a named human with authority reviewed the specific facts before a decision was made. That question is asked of institutions in the public register. The names themselves are not published here.

Letter-writing language for putting that question on the record remains in [`REGISTER.md`](./REGISTER.md).
