# Case study: Equita Ltd

**Sector:** Enforcement agent  
**Finding:** NULL  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Period:** 2026

## What happened

Multiple enforcement statements were issued across linked council tax and parking matters. The challenge asked whether a named human had reviewed each file before enforcement, and whether the underlying orders and notices could be produced.

## The test

Was each enforcement step taken after individual consideration of the specific file, or as part of a bulk process?

## Outcome

The matters were challenged. At the last public write-up, no certified orders, compliant notices, or a substantive answer on human review had been produced. The public register records Equita Ltd as NULL.
