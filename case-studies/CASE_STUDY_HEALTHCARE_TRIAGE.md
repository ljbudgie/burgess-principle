# Case study: Automated triage in NHS urgent and emergency care

**Sector:** Healthcare  
**Finding:** NULL at the algorithm stage  
**Status:** Worked example  
**Period:** 2026

This is a worked example. The trust is not named. Patient medical details are not published.

## What happened

An automated triage system assigned an urgency category to a patient before there was a documented clinician review of that patient's specific circumstances. The wait that followed was longer than the category implied. When a clinician later reviewed the patient, further urgent investigation was ordered.

## The test

Did a clinician personally review this patient's facts before the algorithmic category was treated as the triage decision?

## Outcome

The worked example records NULL at the algorithm stage. Even where a clinician later sees the patient, the first decision about waiting time may already have been taken without documented individual review.
