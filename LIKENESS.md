# Likeness

A photograph of a named person is a fact. Using it before that person has verified it is not.

## The rule

No photograph, still, or likeness of Lewis James Burgess is published, supplied, or treated as verified until he has confirmed it by email at lewisjames@theburgessprinciple.com.

That is a reasonable adjustment. It is also the Burgess Principle applied to an image. A named person applies their mind to the specific image before it is used. If no one has done that, the image is NULL.

A stock face, a flag, and a caption are not a person. An AI composite is not a verification.

## What this is not

This is not a claim that a person owns their body in copyright. It is not a ban on reporting. Satire, parody, and criticism stay where the law already puts them. The adjustment is the check before use, and email as the route for that check.

## Denmark, 8 October 2026

A graphic circulating on 8 October 2026 said Denmark was granting citizens ownership of their face, voice, and body.

That is not what was proposed.

Culture Minister Zenia Stampe introduced a bill to amend Danish copyright law so that sharing a lifelike digital imitation of a person's appearance or voice, without consent, can be stopped. It covers the public and artists. It has an exception for caricature, parody, satire, and criticism of society and those in power. It is not in force. It does not transfer ownership of a body.

The useful part is the check. A lifelike copy is not used until the person in it has been asked.

## The UK route already exists

- Editors' Code, clause 1, accuracy, and clause 2, privacy, through IPSO.
- UK GDPR, where an image identifies a person.
- Equality Act 2010, sections 20 and 29, where the person has asked for email and for a check before an image is used.

The Northern Echo file is the worked example. The adjustment on that file is that any photograph of Lewis James Burgess is verified by him before use. The route is email. A caption is not a verification.

## The one question

Who looked at this image, and did the person in it confirm it?

If the answer is a name and a yes, the use is sovereign. If the answer is a template, a stock face, or a model, it is NULL.
