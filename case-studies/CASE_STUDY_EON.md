# Case study: E.ON Next

**Sector:** Energy  
**Finding:** NULL  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Period:** 2025–2026

## What happened

Forced entry took place under a warrant. The warrant was unsigned on its face. The magistrates' court process later confirmed that energy warrants were processed in batches and granted en bloc, with dip sampling rather than individual scrutiny of each file. Field notes recorded that the customer was deaf, yet enforcement proceeded without a reasonable adjustment.

## The test

Did a named human with authority review the specific facts, including the accessibility record, before entry was authorised and carried out?

## Outcome

A settlement was later reached with the company. An Energy Ombudsman process was still active at the last public write-up. The central evidential point already on the public record is that the warrant process was bulk-operated, not individually scrutinised. The public register records E.ON Next as NULL.
