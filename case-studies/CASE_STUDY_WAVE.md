# Case study: Wave Utilities

**Sector:** Water retail  
**Finding:** NULL at referral, SOVEREIGN at resolution  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Period:** 2025

## What happened

Wave Utilities referred water accounts to a debt collection agent through an automated threshold process. Attendance and disconnection threats followed before anyone was shown to have reviewed the live balances or the customer's request for email-only communication as a reasonable adjustment under the Equality Act 2010.

## The test

The question asked was whether any individual at the collection agent had verified the current account position before attending or threatening disconnection.

## Outcome

When a named person in the company's complex-queries team reviewed the accounts, the matter was resolved in a single exchange. The company confirmed that the referrals had been triggered automatically. Enforcement stopped, the accounts were corrected, and email-only reasonable-adjustment notes were recorded.

This remains the clearest completed public example in the repository of a NULL process becoming SOVEREIGN once a real person looked.
